Safety Guidance

🔋 Batteries

Do not puncture, crush, burn or mix damaged batteries. Isolate and hand over through authorized channels.

🖥 CRT/LCD

Avoid breaking screens. Use gloves and safe handling; send to authorized recyclers.

🔥 No Burning

Do not burn cables, plastic or e-waste. Use formal recycling channels.

🧤 PPE

Use gloves, closed footwear and eye protection when handling hazardous scrap.
